## Latest story direction

The earlier door-and-gold-key concept evolved into a bedtime book story. Start with the mother closing the book and putting it on the bedside table, switching off the lamp, giving a goodnight kiss and leaving. The book then glows and opens; magical floral elements travel toward the wall and form the MERA decoration. The user requested gold MERA lettering and an ornate key motif on the book cover, rather than a loose key resting on the book. The first frame of the prior storyboard was rejected; the original storyboard is unavailable to identify its exact framing.

## Implemented concept

Four scroll-linked text chapters, a continuous camera-like zoom on the available image, gradual dimming and a warm glow. Scrolling backwards reverses the progress. This demonstrates rhythm and art direction, not the missing character/book animation. Reduced-motion preferences and disabled JavaScript expose a readable static sequence. The existing homepage remains the catalogue destination.
